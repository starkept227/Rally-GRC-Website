How RALLY GRC works with you

Compliance work goes wrong when scope is vague, ownership is unclear and nobody knows what “done” looks like. Every RALLY GRC engagement is built to prevent that.

From first call to final readout

  1. Strategy call. A 30-minute conversation about what prompted the request, who is involved, what is at stake and when you need an answer.
  2. Recommendation. You receive a clear recommendation on the right starting point—Ready, Build or Lead—with a defined scope, timeline and investment. If another approach fits better, we say so.
  3. Agreement and kickoff. A written agreement confirms scope, deliverables and confidentiality. We then confirm who owns what on your side and how decisions will get made.
  4. Delivery. We do the work with your team, with regular progress updates and decisions raised early rather than at the end.
  5. Executive readout and handoff. You receive the finished deliverables, a prioritized plan and a clear view of what happens next. The work product is yours to use.

How we work

  • Right-sized. Controls and governance scaled to your stage, customers and risk—not a template built for a much larger company.
  • Decisions over documents. Leaders get concise choices and implications, not control inventories.
  • Evidence from normal work. Programs designed so proof is produced by the way your team already operates.
  • Capability that stays. We build ownership and repeatable routines inside your team, so progress does not depend on us.
  • Honest about limits. We advise; we do not audit, attest or certify, and we do not guarantee audit outcomes.

What we ask of you

  • A named executive sponsor who can make decisions
  • Access to the people who own security, IT, HR, engineering and legal processes
  • The documents and evidence you already have—even if incomplete
  • Prompt answers when a decision is blocking progress
Confidentiality.

We ask only for the access and information an engagement needs, and we handle it under a written confidentiality agreement.

Which engagement fits

  • Ready — a customer, investor or partner has asked for proof and you need a credible plan.
  • Build — compliance activity is scattered, findings are open or privacy and platforms must mature.
  • Lead — you need senior GRC ownership before a full-time executive hire.

Common questions

Do you replace our auditor?

No. We are independent of your audit. We help you get ready for it, work through findings and build the program around it.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We start from your current processes and platforms, including ServiceNow and OneTrust, and recommend changes only when they solve a real problem.

What if we are not sure where to start?

That is what the strategy call is for. Begin with a focused diagnostic, then commit only to the path your priorities justify.
