Privacy Statement

We may collect limited information to improve your experience, including:

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

How We Protect Your Information

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

We do not sell or share your personal information. We may share data only with trusted service providers who support our operations (such as HoneyBook, which powers our strategy-call scheduling, and email or analytics platforms), and only as necessary.

Your Choices

Updates to This Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement periodically. The “Last Updated” date will reflect the most recent revision.
